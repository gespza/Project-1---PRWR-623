# Reviewing an oral history transcript for NeTIA
This documentation helps TU students working with GIVE successfully review an oral history transcript for NeTIA's Oral History Project.
## The communication problem
When TU students who are new to GIVE or working with NeTIA are tasked with reviewing an oral history transcript, it is often their first time doing so. The immediate assumption might be to solely check for accuracy. While checking for accuracy may be part of the process, it is only one consideration of many.
## Intended audience
This guide will be beneficial to students working with GIVE who:
* May be new to working with NeTIA
* May be reviewing an oral history transcript for the first time
* Need to better understand the transcript review process for NeTIA's Oral History Project
## Outcomes of using this guide
By following the procedures laid out in this guide, GIVE students will be able to successfully:
* Understand the goals of NeTIA's Oral History Project
* Review an oral history transcript linked to NeTIA
* Note any uncertainies in a transcript for future reviewers
>**Important Note:** This documentation is not meant to reflect the universal protocols for reviewing all oral history transcripts. All editing decisions are linked to the goals and purposes of that specific project.
## Previous documentation
During the preparation of NeTIA's Oral History Project in 2025, a [transcript review guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1qwaWklws4RVO1BmUbCCJMztSOvKA69muSgeC1wFi8fk/edit?usp=sharing) was created as a way to streamline the process. However, as the project has continued and grown, changes to the review process have not been reflected in the original 2025 guide. This documentation is meant to lay out the most up to date transcript review process. The updated process is less time-consuming while still keeping in mind the needs of the project.
